I had been writing Rx in C# for a couple of years and wanted to know
whether observable streams felt as good in a browser as they did on the
CLR, so I spent a couple of months of evenings in my own time pushing
RxJS into React components. They did, mostly, and the same reactive
thread runs through the Coursera reactive programming course I took
around then. Seven people starred it, which suggests I was not the only
one wondering.
