My first Elixir commits are from December 2014, and 2015 was the year I
learned the language in the open. BeerExpert is one of those exercises,
a small expert system built of an evening because writing something with
rules in it teaches you more about a new language than another kata
does. The Elixir and OTP posts in this site's archive from 2014 and 2015
are the other half of the same habit. It picked up 14 stars, which for a
month of practice I will happily take.
