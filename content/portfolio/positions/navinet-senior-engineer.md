Medical systems in a polyglot of C#, Ruby and a sprinkle of Python; just
over a year exclusively on mobile, from iOS and Android down to some
ancient Windows Mobile devices.

The bit I still think about is the test grammar. Guy Steele's [*Growing a
Language*](/growing-a-test-language/) argues you should start a language small and let its users grow
it, and a test suite turned out to be exactly the place to try that: a
tiny core of primitives, and a vocabulary that testers extended by
defining new words from old ones.
