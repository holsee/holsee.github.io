Most of what a coding agent produces is decided before you type the
prompt: permissions, hooks, subagent definitions, what it remembers and
how. The deck walks that configuration surface as the first step towards
agentic systems proper. Its animated title cycles to *A Primer on
Building Agentic Systems*, which is the truer name. Hand-built HTML
slides, speaker notes included.
