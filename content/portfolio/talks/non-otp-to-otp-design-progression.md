The best way I know to teach OTP is to start without it: solve a problem
with spawn, send and receive, then let the pain points introduce
GenServer and supervisors one at a time. The talk material is on GitHub
as [craic_stack](https://github.com/holsee/craic_stack), which tells you
roughly which city it was written in.
